Super Suite with Jacuzzi — a room for two guests

If you're looking for a room with a jacuzzi in Tbilisi and want to relax somewhere calm, comfortable and private, Hotel Agava offers the Super Suite with Jacuzzi, designed for two guests. It's a good choice for couples who would rather spend their time somewhere cosier and more distinctive than an ordinary hotel room. Here you'll find a King-size bed and a private jacuzzi used only by the guests staying in the room. That means you can unwind in peace, with no other guests around, and use the jacuzzi whenever it suits you. Of Hotel Agava's two jacuzzi rooms, the Super Suite with Jacuzzi is the one with the King-size bed, and it particularly suits a couple who want a comfortable, cosy space.

What you'll find in the room

The Super Suite's main advantage is its private jacuzzi, but the room also has everything else you need to relax. Guests have air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, a private bathroom and a King-size bed. The room gives two people plenty of space while keeping a cosy feel. It's especially well suited to guests who don't just want somewhere to sleep but plan to spend time in the room itself. You can relax, enjoy the jacuzzi and spend a pleasant evening without leaving the hotel.

A jacuzzi room for a special day

When choosing a hotel for a couple in Tbilisi, what matters is not only how clean and comfortable the room is, but also the setting you'll spend your time in. If you're planning to celebrate a special day, a jacuzzi room is a good choice. You can mark an anniversary or a birthday here, plan a romantic evening, or simply step away from your everyday surroundings and relax together. The jacuzzi is right inside the room, so the space stays entirely yours. This matters especially to guests who prefer a private, quiet setting to a shared spa area.

Super Suite or Jacuzzi Suite — which to choose

Hotel Agava has just two rooms with a private jacuzzi, and both are designed for two guests. The difference between them lies in the bed, the view and the price. The Super Suite with Jacuzzi has a King-size bed and costs 400 GEL. The Jacuzzi Suite is 30 m², with a large double bed and a city view, and costs 300 GEL. If a King-size bed and the way the room is furnished matter most to you, the Super Suite with Jacuzzi is a good choice. If you'd prefer a roomier space, a city view and a lower price, take a look at the Jacuzzi Suite. So you can decide by what you value more: the bed and the room's fittings, or extra space and a view.

Relaxing in one place, without a separate spa

A jacuzzi room is also convenient when you don't want to plan your break across several different places. There's no need to find a separate spa or travel somewhere else. You can arrive at the hotel, settle into the room and spend the rest of your time at ease. That's why this option appeals both to couples living in Tbilisi who want a change of scene and to visitors to the city who are looking for extra comfort in a hotel.

Location — Dighomi, 161 Beliashvili Street

Hotel Agava is in Tbilisi, in Dighomi, at 161 Akaki Beliashvili Street. It's a convenient location for anyone who wants to stay in a relatively quiet part of the city. Guests have free parking, which is especially handy if you're arriving in your own car. There's also a 24/7 reception, so you can turn to the hotel staff at any time if you need anything. If you live in Tbilisi, you don't have to travel far to book a jacuzzi room. You can make even a single evening feel different and give yourself a short break from the daily routine.

Booking in advance

Because Hotel Agava has only two rooms with a private jacuzzi, booking ahead for your preferred date is recommended. Especially at weekends, during holidays and on popular dates, the rooms can be booked up quickly. If you're planning to celebrate an anniversary, a birthday or another important day, it's best to check availability in advance. But if what matters most to you is a peaceful setting, a private jacuzzi and a comfortable space, Hotel Agava's Super Suite with Jacuzzi is a good choice for a couple. Here you can relax, get away from your usual surroundings and have a lovely time in a cosy room made for two.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between the Super Suite with Jacuzzi and the Jacuzzi Suite?

The Super Suite with Jacuzzi has a King-size bed and costs 400 GEL. The Jacuzzi Suite is 30 m², with a large double bed and a city view, and costs 300 GEL. Both are for two guests.

Is the jacuzzi used only by the room's guests?

Yes. The jacuzzi is inside the room itself, so only the guests staying in that room use it, whenever they like.

Is breakfast included in the room price?

No. Breakfast is ordered separately — 30 GEL per guest, buffet from 08:00 to 11:00.

How do I pay, and what time is check-in?

Payment is on site, in cash only. Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00.
