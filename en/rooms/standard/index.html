Standard Room for two guests in Tbilisi

The Standard Room at Hotel Agava is designed for two guests and is a good choice for anyone who needs clean, comfortable and practical accommodation in Tbilisi. The room is 22 m² and has one large bed. This option is especially convenient for a short visit, a business trip or a couple who need the essential comforts during their stay and for whom extra space isn't the top priority. The room is big enough for two guests to store their things comfortably and relax in a calm setting at the end of the day.

Size and space

The Standard Room is a good choice for guests who spend most of the day out in the city and use the hotel mainly for resting and sleeping. If you're coming to Tbilisi for business meetings, to attend an event or for a visit of several days, a 22 m² room provides a practical setting. Without surplus space, you get all the essentials needed for a comfortable stay. The large bed gives two guests a comfortable night's rest, while the room's compact layout keeps the space cosy.

What you'll find in the room

The room has air conditioning, free Wi-Fi and a private bathroom. Air conditioning lets you keep the room at a comfortable temperature, which is especially important in the summer months. You can use the free Wi-Fi for personal needs as well as for work, looking things up or planning the next day. And a private bathroom adds peace of mind and comfort, since everything you need is right in the room.

The Standard Room is also convenient if you're staying several nights but don't need a large space. When travelling, what often matters most is cleanliness, a calm setting, a comfortable bed and the things that make everyday life easier. In that case 22 m² can be perfectly enough for two people. If you mostly come back to the room in the evening and are out in the city during the day, booking a larger room isn't always necessary.

For guests in Tbilisi on business, the Standard Room can be a practical choice precisely because of its simple, comfortable format. After a working day you can come back to a calm setting, use the internet to deal with whatever needs doing and then rest. If you don't need a workspace or a very large room at the hotel, the Standard Room gives you the comfort you need without extra space.

This room is also a good choice for couples who come to Tbilisi for several days and plan to see the city. You can spend the day out in the city and come back to the hotel in the evening to relax. The large bed and private bathroom create a comfortable setting for two, and the room's size meets all the essential needs without requiring a very large space.

Parking and 24/7 reception

Guests at Hotel Agava have the hotel's own parking. This added advantage is especially convenient for anyone arriving in Tbilisi in their own car. You don't need to look for a parking space separately and can leave the car close to the hotel. This is practical on both short visits and stays of several days, especially if you plan to get around the city in your own car.

The hotel also has a 24/7 reception, which lets guests plan their arrival time more freely. If you're arriving in Tbilisi late in the evening or at night, you can still reach the hotel staff. This matters especially when a delayed flight, a hold-up on the road or something else changes your planned arrival time. In this respect, 24/7 check-in adds extra comfort.

The Standard Room's main advantage is its practicality. It's designed for guests who don't need a very large room but want a clean, tidy space with all the essentials. A room like this can be an especially good choice for one or two nights, but it's also convenient for longer stays for anyone who doesn't see a large floor area as a must.

Other rooms at Hotel Agava

If you'd like more free space in the room, take a look at the 28 m² Deluxe Room. The Deluxe Room is roomier than the Standard Room and a good choice for two guests who prefer having more space. This option is especially convenient on a visit of several days, if you have more luggage or simply prefer a roomier setting.

For even more space, you can choose the 36 m² Superior Suite. It's one of the most spacious options for two at Hotel Agava and suits guests for whom room size matters a great deal. If the Standard Room's 22 m² isn't enough for you, the Deluxe Room and the Superior Suite let you choose by the amount of space you want.

For couples planning a break that's different from an ordinary hotel room, Hotel Agava also has a jacuzzi room. You might choose this option for a special evening, an anniversary, a birthday or simply when a private jacuzzi and a different setting matter to you. The Standard Room, meanwhile, is the more practical choice for an everyday or short visit.

Different types of room let you decide according to the purpose of your visit. If you're staying in Tbilisi for one or a few days and won't spend much time in the room, the Standard Room may be perfectly enough. If you want more space, you can choose the Deluxe Room or the Superior Suite, and for a special break, consider a jacuzzi room.

Hotel Agava is in Tbilisi, in Dighomi, and offers several types of room for two guests. Among them, the Standard Room is the most practical option for guests who want the essential comforts, a calm setting and everything they need. 22 m², a large bed, air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, a private bathroom, the hotel's parking and 24/7 reception make it convenient for both leisure and business visits.

If you're looking for a room for two in Tbilisi and don't need a large space, Hotel Agava's Standard Room is a good choice. Here you can settle in comfortably, spend the day in the city and come back to a calm setting in the evening. The room's simple, practical format especially suits guests who want the comfort they need during their stay without surplus space.
