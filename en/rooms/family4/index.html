Family Room for 4 guests in Tbilisi

Hotel Agava offers a spacious family room for four guests, a good choice when visiting Tbilisi with family or a small group. The room is 38 m² and has two beds. Its size lets four guests stay comfortably in one room while still having free space to relax. A room like this is especially convenient when the whole family wants to stay together in one space and there's no need to book several separate rooms.

The family room suits two parents and two children well. Staying in one room makes many things simpler on a family trip, because everyone is together and you don't have to go between different rooms inside the hotel. This matters especially when you're travelling with children and want everyone to be able to relax comfortably in one space at the end of the day. The room can also suit a group of four who would rather share one room in Tbilisi.

Size and space

38 m² gives a room for four guests plenty of space. When several people share a room, size matters especially, because simply fitting in the beds isn't enough for comfort. Guests need room for luggage, for moving around freely and for putting away everyday things. A spacious family room gives you exactly that and stays comfortable over a stay of several days.

What you'll find in the room

The room has air conditioning, a fridge, free Wi-Fi and a private bathroom. These are especially practical for families staying at the hotel for several days. The fridge lets you keep drinks, food or other things you need, which is particularly handy when travelling with children. Air conditioning helps keep the room at a comfortable temperature, and you can use the free Wi-Fi for relaxing, for work, for looking things up or for planning the next part of your trip.

A private bathroom adds comfort for four guests, because there's no need to leave the room and every family member can have their own private space. When you're staying several days, details like these have a real impact on the overall experience. That's why a spacious family room works well both for a short visit and for a relatively long stay.

Hotel Agava's family room can also be a good choice for anyone coming to Tbilisi for work or personal reasons and travelling with family members. When travelling with family, the main aim is often to keep the hotel arrangements as simple as possible. One room for four guests makes this easier, because everyone stays together and nothing extra needs organising inside the hotel.

Parking and 24/7 reception

Another advantage for families is the hotel's own parking. If you're coming to Tbilisi by car, you won't need to look for a parking space separately. This is especially convenient when you have a lot of luggage or are getting around with children. Parking the car close to the hotel makes both arrival and departure easier and adds comfort to getting around day to day.

The hotel also has a 24/7 reception, which lets you plan your arrival time more freely. If you're arriving in Tbilisi late in the evening or at night, check-in is still available. This is a real advantage for families and groups, since travel times don't always go to plan. A delay on the road, a late flight or other circumstances can change your arrival time, so a 24/7 reception adds peace of mind.
