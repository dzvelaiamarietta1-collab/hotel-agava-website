Spacious Superior Suite in Tbilisi

If you're looking for an especially spacious and comfortable room for two in Tbilisi, Hotel Agava offers the Superior Suite, which is 36 m². This option is designed for two guests and is one of the most spacious double rooms at the hotel. The room has a large bed, and the extra space lets you settle in more freely and relax in comfort during your stay. The Superior Suite is a good choice both for couples and for anyone staying in Tbilisi for several days who puts particular value on room size.

Size and space

36 m² is especially convenient for guests who don't like small, compact rooms. The larger space lets you store luggage and everyday things freely and move around the room without feeling cramped. This matters especially when you're staying more than one night and plan to spend some time in the room. On a visit of several days, a spacious room has a real effect on overall comfort and makes time in the room more pleasant.

What you'll find in the room

Guests in the Superior Suite have air conditioning, free Wi-Fi and a private bathroom. These provide everyday comfort on both short and longer visits. Air conditioning lets you keep the room at the temperature you like, which is especially important in summer. You can use the free Wi-Fi for relaxing, looking things up, watching videos or working. And a private bathroom adds peace of mind and independence.

The Superior Suite is a good choice for couples who want to come back to a spacious, quiet room after exploring Tbilisi. You can spend the day out in the city and return to the hotel in the evening to relax in comfort. A spacious room is especially pleasant when the trip lasts several days and the hotel is no longer just somewhere to spend the night. In that case it also matters how freely you can be in the room and how comfortably the space is laid out.

This room is also a practical option for guests in Tbilisi on business. If you're here for meetings, work or other business and staying at the hotel for several nights, more space makes resting more comfortable. Coming back to a calm setting at the end of the day and being able to move freely in the room matters, especially after a busy day. Free internet lets you carry on working or join an online meeting if you need to.

Choosing the Superior Suite can also be convenient when you have a fair amount of luggage. In a small room, a few bags or suitcases often eat up the space, while 36 m² makes this much less of a problem. You can store your things so that there's still enough room to move around and the room doesn't look cluttered.

Parking and 24/7 reception

Guests at Hotel Agava can also use the hotel's own parking. This is especially convenient for anyone arriving in Tbilisi by car or planning to get around the city in their own car. You don't need to look for a parking space in advance and can leave the car close to the hotel. This makes both arrival and departure easier, as well as getting around day to day.

The hotel has a 24/7 reception, which lets guests plan their arrival time more freely. If there's a delay on the road, your flight is rescheduled or you reach Tbilisi late in the evening, you can contact the hotel staff at any time. This is especially reassuring when travelling to an unfamiliar city and you want to be sure you'll be able to check in even if you arrive late.

If 36 m² isn't essential for you and you're looking for a slightly smaller room, take a look at the Deluxe Room, which is 28 m². The Deluxe Room is a good choice for guests who want more space than a standard room but don't need the size of the Superior Suite. The main difference between these two options is precisely the size, so you can choose based on how much the extra free space matters to you.

Other rooms at Hotel Agava

If you prefer a more compact and practical stay, you can choose the Standard Room. The Standard Room is a good option for a short visit or for guests who come back to the hotel mainly to sleep and for whom a large space isn't the main requirement. The Superior Suite, on the other hand, is more suitable when you want more freedom and comfort in the room.

For a special evening, Hotel Agava also offers a jacuzzi room or the Jacuzzi Suite. These options are especially appealing to couples who want something different from an ordinary hotel room and for whom a private jacuzzi is an important detail. The Jacuzzi room (Super Suite with Jacuzzi) offers guests a King-size bed, while the Jacuzzi Suite (deluxe room with a jacuzzi) is a 30 m² room with a city view.

If your main priority is simply a spacious, comfortable room, the Superior Suite is a good choice. 36 m² gives two guests plenty of freedom and lets you plan your time in the room more comfortably. Here you can both relax and spend several days in peace without a small space making you uncomfortable.

Hotel Agava is in Tbilisi, in Dighomi, and offers guests different types of rooms, which makes choosing easier. You can pick the more compact Standard Room, the roomier Deluxe Room, the even larger Superior Suite or a jacuzzi option for a special break. This choice lets you select a room according to the length of your visit, its purpose and the comfort you want.

If you're looking for a spacious room for two in Tbilisi and free space, a large bed and everything you need for everyday comfort matter to you, Hotel Agava's Superior Suite is a good choice. 36 m², air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, a private bathroom, the hotel's parking and 24/7 reception create a setting that works both for a short break and for a visit of several days.

Frequently asked questions

How big is the Superior Suite and what bed does it have?

The Superior Suite is 36 m², with a King-size bed, for two guests.

How much does the Superior Suite cost?

The Superior Suite costs 300 GEL per night.

What's the difference between the Superior Suite and the Deluxe Room?

The main difference is size: the Superior Suite is 36 m² and costs 300 GEL, the Deluxe Room is 28 m² and costs 250 GEL.

Is breakfast included in the room price?

No. Breakfast is ordered separately — 30 GEL per guest, buffet from 08:00 to 11:00.
