Two-Room Suite for 5 guests in Tbilisi

When you travel with a large family or a group of friends, a single big room isn't always enough. In that case it matters that everyone has a comfortable place to rest while the group still stays together in one space. Hotel Agava offers a 45 m² Two-Room Suite, designed for five guests and made up of two separate rooms. The suite has three beds, so you can stay in one suite and still have more personal space.

The two-room layout is especially convenient for a large family. If you're travelling with children, two separate rooms make it easier to divide up the space for resting. Parents can stay in one room while the children settle into the other. This is especially comfortable in the evening and at night, when different family members keep different routines. Having everyone together in a single room can sometimes be tiring, so the Two-Room Suite creates a calmer, more relaxed setting.

Size and space

45 m² gives a suite for five guests plenty of space. When several people stay at a hotel together, what matters is not only the number of beds but also how freely you can move around and store luggage. In the Two-Room Suite the space is divided into two parts, which adds to overall comfort and lets guests keep their things in better order.

The suite has three beds, which makes it easier to accommodate five guests. It's a practical option both for a large family and for a group of friends who want to stay at the hotel together. Separate rooms are especially convenient when members of the group want to be in one space but don't want to share one room completely. In that case two rooms strike a good balance between shared and private space.

What you'll find in the room

The Two-Room Suite has air conditioning, a fridge, free Wi-Fi and a private bathroom. These matter especially on a visit of several days, when you use the hotel room every day and comfort isn't just about spending the night. Air conditioning helps keep the rooms at a pleasant temperature, you can use the free Wi-Fi for relaxing as well as for work or planning your trip, and a private bathroom adds convenience for five guests.

The fridge is especially practical for families and groups staying at the hotel for several days. You can keep drinks, food, things the children need or anything else that has to stay cool. When five people share a suite, details like these make everyday life simpler and add comfort to your stay.

Hotel Agava's Two-Room Suite can also be a good choice for anyone staying in Tbilisi for several days or longer. For a large group, having two rooms makes the daily routine much easier. You can relax in one space and use the other room for sleeping or quieter time when needed. This layout is especially convenient when there are children in the group or guests keep different rest routines.

A two-room suite is also practical for a group of friends. If five of you are travelling to Tbilisi together, booking several separate rooms isn't always necessary. One large Two-Room Suite lets everyone stay in one place while still keeping enough space for each other. This makes both everyday communication and planning the day together easier.

Parking and 24/7 reception

Guests at Hotel Agava have the hotel's own parking. This is an especially important advantage for a large family or group arriving in Tbilisi by car. Moving several people and their luggage often takes extra organising, so being able to park close to the hotel makes arrival and departure easier. And if you're getting around in your own car, you won't need to hunt for a parking space every day.

The hotel also has a 24/7 reception. This is especially convenient for a large group, since arrival times can't always be planned exactly when travelling. If there's a delay on the road, your flight is late or you reach Tbilisi late in the evening, you can still reach the hotel staff. This lets you plan your journey more calmly and depend less on your arrival time.

The Two-Room Suite is also a good choice for families who need extra space because of the children. When travelling with children you often bring more belongings, clothes and other things you need. 45 m² and two separate rooms give you more freedom to store them without the space feeling cluttered. You notice this especially on a visit of several days, when being in the suite becomes part of your daily routine.

Other rooms at Hotel Agava

If you don't need to accommodate five people, you can choose the Family Room for 4 guests. This option is designed for four people and is convenient both for two parents with two children and for a small group. And if there are only three of you, take a look at the Family Room for 3 guests, a more compact option that meets the needs of three people.

When choosing a room, it's worth considering not only the number of guests but also how much separate spaces matter to you. For four or three people, one room may be perfectly enough, but for five guests two rooms are far more convenient. This is especially true when you're staying several nights and want every guest to have a comfortable setting.

Being able to choose rooms for different numbers of guests at Hotel Agava lets you match your accommodation to the needs of your family or group. If there are three or four of you, you can choose the matching family room. For five guests, the Two-Room Suite is the more practical option, because it offers both a large shared space and two separate rooms in one suite.

If you're looking for a room for five people in Tbilisi and want everyone to stay together while still having more personal space, Hotel Agava's Two-Room Suite is a good choice. 45 m², two separate rooms, three beds, air conditioning, a fridge, free Wi-Fi and a private bathroom make accommodating a large family or group simpler and more comfortable. And the hotel's own parking and 24/7 reception make organising your journey and check-in even easier.
