Rooms for two guests

For a couple or a solo traveller there are four options, from the simplest to the jacuzzi suites:

  • Standard room — 22 m², the most affordable choice for a night or two
  • Lux — more space and better furnishings
  • Superlux — the largest room for two
  • Jacuzzi suite · Super suite with jacuzzi — see the jacuzzi rooms page

Family rooms for 3–5 guests

Travelling as a family or a group? There are three options. More detail on the family hotel page:

  • Family room for 3 guests
  • Family room for 4 guests
  • Two-bedroom suite for 5 guests — two separate rooms

Suites with a jacuzzi

Two rooms have a jacuzzi — the Jacuzzi suite and the Super suite with jacuzzi, both for two guests. They are usually booked for anniversaries, birthdays, or simply a weekend that should feel different. Full description on the jacuzzi rooms in Tbilisi page.

How to choose a room

A simple guide: two guests, short stay — the standard room. Two guests, special occasion — a jacuzzi suite. A family with children — a family room for 3 or 4. Two couples or a large family — the two-bedroom suite, which has two separate rooms.

If you are not sure, call us on +995 597 12 12 12 or message us on WhatsApp — we will check your dates and suggest what fits.

What 21 rooms means in practice

Agava is not a chain hotel, and that is deliberate. Twenty-one rooms means there is no queue at reception, a request is answered by a particular person rather than a call centre, and if you are arriving at night they already know you are coming.

There is a flip side, and we would rather say it: rooms fill quickly — at weekends, over holidays and through the summer especially. If your dates are fixed, book ahead, because at the last minute there may be only one type of room left.

Arriving, leaving, and turning up late

Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00. Reception is staffed 24 hours, so a night arrival is not a problem — just tell us in advance so we know to expect you.

Early check-in and late check-out are both possible but charged, and they depend on how full the hotel is that day. Call +995 597 12 12 12 for the exact amount.

One thing worth knowing in advance: the hotel has no luggage room. If you are staying in the city after check-out, your bags come with you — which is why a late check-out is often the more practical answer on that day.

Booking and payment

Payment is taken at the hotel, in cash only — we do not accept cards, so plan to have lari with you. No online prepayment is required.

Booking direct has one practical advantage: you ask the question and get the answer immediately — an extra bed, a child's age, a late arrival, two rooms next to each other. Call +995 597 12 12 12 or message us on WhatsApp.

What the hotel is, and what it is not

So that nothing surprises you on arrival: Agava is a comfortable mid-range hotel built around a clean room, free parking in the yard and a quiet night. There is no full-menu restaurant, swimming pool, spa or gym.

If that is what you are after, the centre has better options. If what you need is a clean, quiet room, a space for the car and somebody at the desk at any hour, that is exactly what this is. The 4.8 on Google comes from meeting that expectation rather than a grander one.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How many rooms does the hotel have?

Hotel Agava has 21 rooms — standard rooms, lux and superlux, two jacuzzi suites and family rooms for 3–5 guests.

Which room is the largest?

The two-bedroom suite, which sleeps five and consists of two separate rooms. For two guests the superlux is the most spacious.

What is included in the room rate?

The rate covers accommodation, air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, a private bathroom and daily housekeeping. Breakfast is not included in the room rate — you order it separately. Add a note to your booking or arrange it with reception on arrival.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 13:00, check-out until 12:00. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged in advance, subject to availability.

How do I pay?

Payment is taken at the hotel — in cash only; cards are not accepted. No prepayment is required for an online booking.

Can I smoke?

Smoking is not allowed in the rooms. You can smoke outside, in the covered part of the courtyard. There is no fine.

Can an extra bed be added?

No. Neither an extra bed nor a baby cot is added in any room. Choose the room by the number of guests — there are rooms for three, four and five.

Do children cost extra?

No. The price is per room, not per person — what matters is that the number of guests does not exceed the room's capacity. Breakfast is ordered separately, at 30 GEL per guest.
