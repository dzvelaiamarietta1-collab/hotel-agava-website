Family Room for 3 guests in Tbilisi

Finding one comfortable room for three people makes travelling much easier, especially when you're travelling with family or want to stay in one space with friends. Hotel Agava offers a 32 m² family room designed for three guests, with two beds. The room is a good choice both for two parents and a child and for three adults who want to share one comfortable room in Tbilisi. There's enough space for guests to relax freely and feel at ease during a stay of several days.

One of the main advantages of a family room is that all three guests stay in one space and there's no need to book an extra room. This is especially convenient for families who want to stay in one room with their child and be close to each other throughout the visit. The two beds make it easier to divide up the space for resting and to use the room the way you need. The same option is practical for three adults travelling together who don't want to take separate rooms at the hotel.

What you'll find in the room

Guests have air conditioning, free Wi-Fi and a private bathroom in the room. These matter especially when you're staying several days and want everyday comforts without leaving the room. A private bathroom adds convenience for three guests, while air conditioning helps keep the room at a pleasant temperature in the summer months. Free Wi-Fi lets you use the internet for relaxing, for work or for planning your trip.

Size and space

32 m² is a comfortable size for a room designed for three guests. The space is laid out so that guests don't feel cramped and don't come back to the room only to sleep. If you're travelling with a child, extra space is especially important, because family trips usually mean more belongings and luggage. In a roomier space they're easier to store, and moving around day to day is more comfortable too.

Hotel Agava's family room can be a good choice for a break of several days, a work trip or a longer stay in Tbilisi. When three people travel together, choosing one room makes planning the trip simpler and the stay more comfortable. Family members or friends can stay in one space without having to go between rooms inside the hotel. This is especially convenient for families with children, as parents can be in the same room as their child and feel more at ease throughout the day.

Parking and 24/7 reception

Another advantage for families is the hotel's own parking. If you're coming to Tbilisi in your own car, you won't need to look for a parking space separately. This is practical especially when you have a lot of luggage or are travelling with a small child. Being able to leave the car close to the hotel makes both arrival and departure much easier. Guests also have a 24/7 reception, which is convenient if you arrive late at night or early in the morning.

A 24/7 reception is also a real advantage for guests whose travel times aren't fixed in advance. If a flight, a delay on the road or anything else means you reach Tbilisi late, checking in at the hotel won't be a problem. This is particularly reassuring for families, since arriving at the planned time isn't always possible when travelling with a child.

Other rooms at Hotel Agava

If there are four of you rather than three, Hotel Agava has a roomier Family Room for 4 guests. This option lets the whole family stay in one room with extra space as well. If you're a larger family or travelling as a group of five, take a look at the Two-Room Suite, which is made up of two separate rooms. It's especially convenient when guests need more personal space or you'd like the sleeping and relaxing areas to be separate from each other.

Choosing a hotel room by the number of guests is more practical, because you know from the start how many people the space is designed for and what kind of arrangement you'll have. At Hotel Agava you can choose between rooms for three, four or more guests. That way there's no need to book several rooms just so every member of the family has a comfortable place to stay.

The family room for three guests is an especially good choice for anyone who wants to stay in one room but doesn't want a very small space. 32 m², two beds, a private bathroom, air conditioning and free Wi-Fi give you what a family or group visit needs. If you're coming to Tbilisi for a few days, you can explore the city during the day and come back to a comfortable room in the evening, where the whole family can relax together.

Hotel Agava is in Tbilisi, in Dighomi, and a family room can be convenient both for visitors to the city and for anyone planning to spend a few days in Tbilisi with family or friends. The hotel's own parking, 24/7 reception and rooms for different numbers of guests make planning your stay easier. If there are three of you and you want to stay comfortably in one space, the 32 m² family room is a practical choice that combines the space you need with the essential comforts in one room.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How many guests is this room for?

The Family Room is for 3 guests — with two beds and 32 m² of space. It suits two parents and a child, or three adults.

How much does the room cost?

The Family Room for 3 guests costs 150 GEL per night.

There are four or five of us — what are our options?

For four guests there's the Family Room for 4 guests, 38 m². For five guests, the Two-Room Suite — 45 m², with two separate rooms.

Is parking free?

Yes, it's free for hotel guests. There are 11 spaces in the yard, so please let us know in advance if you're arriving by car.
