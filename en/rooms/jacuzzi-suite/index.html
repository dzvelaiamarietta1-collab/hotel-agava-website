Jacuzzi Suite in Tbilisi — 30 m², with a city view

If you're looking for a room with a jacuzzi in Tbilisi and plenty of space matters to you as well, Hotel Agava offers the Jacuzzi Suite (a deluxe room with a jacuzzi), designed for two guests. It's a 30 m² room with a city view and an especially good choice for couples who want to spend time somewhere calm, comfortable and private. The room works both for celebrating a special day and simply for a break, when you want a change from your everyday surroundings and a more comfortable place to stay. The Jacuzzi Suite combines a private jacuzzi, a large bed and extra free space, which makes time in the room more enjoyable. Guests also have air conditioning, free Wi-Fi and a private bathroom.

Size and space

The Jacuzzi Suite's main advantage is that, along with a private jacuzzi, guests get 30 m² of space and a city view. This matters especially when you plan to spend several hours or a whole evening in the room and don't want it to feel cramped. The large bed, the jacuzzi and a more open layout create a setting where two people can relax in comfort. The room is designed for two guests, so the space stays quiet and private. Only the room's guests use the jacuzzi, which lets you spend your time at your own pace without needing a shared relaxation area.

A room like this is a good choice for a couple who want something different from an ordinary hotel room. You might choose the Jacuzzi Suite for an anniversary, a birthday, an important date or simply a pleasant evening. When you celebrate a special day, it often matters that the room itself sets the mood rather than feeling like just a place to spend the night. A private jacuzzi and a spacious room offer exactly that. After you arrive at the hotel, you can spend the rest of your time in the room, relax and enjoy a quiet evening without planning extra activities in the city.

Hotel Agava has two rooms with a private jacuzzi. The Jacuzzi Suite is 30 m² with a city view, while the Jacuzzi room (Super Suite with Jacuzzi) offers guests a King-size bed. Both rooms are designed for two people. The Jacuzzi Suite costs 300 GEL, the Jacuzzi room 400 GEL. When choosing between them, the key questions are the view, the bed and the price. If you prefer a King-size bed, the Jacuzzi room may suit you better. But if you want a city view and a lower price along with your private jacuzzi, the Jacuzzi Suite is the better choice. So you can decide according to the setting you prefer and your budget.

A spacious room is especially comfortable when you're not going to the hotel just to sleep. If you plan to spend several hours in the room, the extra space makes a real difference to the whole experience. You can spread out more freely, relax and take your time. This is one of the reasons the Jacuzzi Suite can also appeal to couples who live in Tbilisi and don't want to leave the city for a break. Sometimes a comfortable hotel room where you can spend a day or an evening in peace is all it takes for a change of scene.

What you'll find in the room

The Jacuzzi Suite is also a good option for visitors to Tbilisi who want more comfort than usual from their hotel. The private jacuzzi lets you unwind in a calm setting at the end of the day, and the 30 m² room is particularly pleasant for anyone staying several days or who simply doesn't like small spaces. Air conditioning, free Wi-Fi and a private bathroom give guests all the essential comforts they need for a hotel stay.

Hotel Agava is in Tbilisi, in Dighomi. The location suits anyone who wants to stay somewhere relatively quiet while still being within Tbilisi. Guests have free parking, which is especially convenient for people arriving in their own car. There's also a 24/7 reception, so you can reach the hotel staff at any time. These things matter both to visitors to the city and to Tbilisi residents who choose a hotel for a break or to celebrate a special day.

Other rooms at Hotel Agava

If a private jacuzzi isn't essential for you and your main requirement is a spacious, comfortable room, Hotel Agava has other options too. For example, take a look at the Superior Suite or the Deluxe Room. These are a good fit if you care more about room size and overall comfort and a jacuzzi isn't the deciding factor. But if you want both a spacious room and a private jacuzzi in one space, the Jacuzzi Suite brings the two together.

Booking in advance

The Jacuzzi Suite costs 300 GEL and is designed for two guests. Because Hotel Agava has only two rooms with a private jacuzzi, it's best to book ahead for your preferred date. This matters especially at weekends, during holidays and on days when you're planning to celebrate an anniversary, a birthday or another important date. Booking in advance makes it easier to get the room you want.

If you're looking for a room with a jacuzzi and a city view for two in Tbilisi, the Jacuzzi Suite is a good choice for anyone who wants privacy, a city view and a comfortable break in one room. What sets it apart from Hotel Agava's other jacuzzi room is the city view and the lower price. So if you prefer a city view and an affordable price, the Jacuzzi Suite lets you relax, enjoy a private jacuzzi and spend time that matters to you in pleasant surroundings.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between the Jacuzzi Suite and the Super Suite with Jacuzzi?

The Jacuzzi Suite is 30 m², with a large double bed and a city view, and costs 300 GEL. The Super Suite with Jacuzzi has a King-size bed and costs 400 GEL. Both are for two guests.

Is the jacuzzi used only by the room's guests?

Yes. The jacuzzi is inside the room itself, so only the guests staying in that room use it, whenever they like.

Is breakfast included in the room price?

No. Breakfast is ordered separately — 30 GEL per guest, buffet from 08:00 to 11:00.

How do I pay?

Payment is on site, in cash only. Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00.
