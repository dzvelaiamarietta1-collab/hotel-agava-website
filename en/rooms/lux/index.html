Deluxe Room for two guests in Tbilisi

If you're looking for a room for two in Tbilisi that is roomier and more comfortable than a standard one, Hotel Agava offers the 28 m² Deluxe Room with a large bed. It's a good choice for couples, for guests in Tbilisi on business, or for anyone who wants a little more free space during their hotel stay. The room is designed for two guests and creates a calm setting where you can relax comfortably, whether on a short visit or staying for several days.

Size and space

One of the Deluxe Room's main advantages is its size. 28 m² lets you settle into the room freely without feeling cramped. This matters especially when you're not staying at the hotel only to sleep and plan to spend some time in the room. The extra space is handy for storing luggage, putting away everyday things and simply moving around the room more easily.

The room has a large bed, which makes the Deluxe Room especially comfortable for couples. If you're travelling to Tbilisi together and want to stay somewhere peaceful, a room like this lets you get away from the noise of the city at the end of the day and come back to a space that feels comfortable for you. The room is also practical on a business trip, when you have meetings or work during the day and want to rest somewhere calm and orderly in the evening.

What you'll find in the room

Guests in the Deluxe Room have air conditioning, free Wi-Fi and a private bathroom. These provide everyday comfort and matter especially on a visit of several days. Air conditioning lets you keep the room at the temperature you like, free Wi-Fi can be used for both personal and work purposes, and a private bathroom gives guests their own comfortable space.

Other rooms at Hotel Agava

This option is also convenient for couples who come to Tbilisi for several days and plan to see the city. When you stay at a hotel for several nights, the size and comfort of the room become more important, because you use the space every day and want it to be convenient.

Hotel Agava's Deluxe Room can also be a good choice for anyone who doesn't need a very large room but finds the standard option too compact. 28 m² strikes a comfortable balance between the two. The room is spacious enough for two guests without being so large that you end up booking space you don't need.

Parking and 24/7 reception

Hotel Agava is in Tbilisi, in Dighomi. Guests have the hotel's own parking, which is especially convenient when travelling by car. In Tbilisi, finding a parking space is often an issue, so being able to leave the car on the hotel grounds adds extra comfort. This is especially practical for guests who stay in the city for several days and use their car every day.

The hotel also has a 24/7 reception. This lets you plan your arrival more freely and reach the hotel staff even if you arrive late in the evening or at night. If your travel time changes because of something on the way, 24/7 check-in adds peace of mind. This matters both for tourists and for guests in Tbilisi on business.

If more space is what matters most to you and 28 m² isn't enough, take a look at the 36 m² Superior Suite. This option is for guests who want an even more spacious setting and prefer more free space in the room. The Superior Suite can be especially convenient on a longer visit or if room size is particularly important to you.

If instead you're looking for a more compact and practical option, you can choose the Standard Room. The Standard Room is a good choice when you're staying at the hotel mainly to sleep and a large space isn't your top priority. The Deluxe Room, on the other hand, suits guests who want the same kind of stay with a little more space and a more relaxed setting.

Different types of room let you match your choice to the purpose of your visit. If you're staying just one or two nights and need a practical room, the Standard Room may be enough. If you want more space and freedom, the Deluxe Room is a better fit. And if you need an even larger room or extra comfort, you can consider the Superior Suite or a jacuzzi room.

The Deluxe Room is an especially good choice for two guests who care equally about room size and everyday comfort. 28 m², a large bed, a private bathroom, air conditioning and free Wi-Fi give you what you need for both a short visit and a stay of several days. The Deluxe Room is 28 m², with a King-size bed, for two guests.
