Jacuzzi rooms in Tbilisi for couples

If you're looking for a room with a jacuzzi in Tbilisi and want to relax as a couple somewhere calm and private, Hotel Agava offers two different options, both designed for two guests. In both rooms the jacuzzi is right inside the guests' private space, so you don't need to go to a shared area or book a time slot to use it. This is especially convenient for anyone who wants to spend their time in peace and make relaxing in the room more comfortable and private.

Hotel Agava has the Jacuzzi room (Super Suite with Jacuzzi) and the Jacuzzi Suite (deluxe room with a jacuzzi). The main differences between these two rooms are the bed, the view and the price. The Jacuzzi room, with a King-size bed, costs 400 GEL, while the Jacuzzi Suite is a 30 m² room with a city view and costs 300 GEL. Both rooms are designed for two guests and both are meant for a couple's break, but the bed, the view and the price differ.

If you prefer a King-size bed, the Jacuzzi room is a good choice. The room creates a calmer, more private setting. This option is especially convenient for a single evening, to celebrate an anniversary, or when you simply want a change from your everyday surroundings and time for just the two of you. Having a private jacuzzi means the room isn't just a place to spend the night but becomes part of the break itself.

The Jacuzzi Suite is 30 m², with a city view. If a city view and a lower price matter to you, this option is the better fit. 30 m² is especially comfortable when you're staying at the hotel for more than a few hours or want to spend more time in the room on a special day. That's why, when choosing, it matters not only that there's a jacuzzi but also which kind of space suits what you want.

Couples often choose jacuzzi rooms to celebrate important dates. It might be an anniversary, a birthday, a break after the wedding or simply a pleasant evening for which you want a setting different from an ordinary hotel room. When planning a special day, it matters that the room itself sets the right mood and that guests can spend their time in peace without moving around unnecessarily or using shared spaces.

The main advantage of a private jacuzzi is that the space belongs only to the guests in the room. You don't have to share a spa area with other people, and there's no need to book a time in advance. You can plan your time in the room however you like and use the jacuzzi whenever you feel like it. This matters especially to couples who value privacy and a calm setting.

Free Wi-Fi

Both rooms have a private bathroom, air conditioning and free Wi-Fi. These give you all the essential comforts in one space during your stay. Air conditioning helps keep the room at the temperature you like, free Wi-Fi is there for your personal use, and a private bathroom adds extra peace of mind and comfort.

Free parking

Guests at Hotel Agava also have the hotel's own parking. If you're coming to the hotel by car, you can park right on the hotel grounds. This is especially convenient for an evening visit, when you don't want to look for a parking space on the street. Having parking is also comfortable for anyone coming to the hotel from a city other than Tbilisi and travelling in their own car.

24/7 reception

The hotel has a 24/7 reception, which lets guests plan their arrival time more freely. If you're coming to the hotel late in the evening or don't know exactly what time you'll arrive, reception is available all night. This is especially practical when planning a special evening, when you have other plans during the day and the exact time you reach the hotel may change.

Rooms for two guests

If you want a break as a couple but a private jacuzzi isn't essential for you, take a look at the hotel's options for couples. There you can compare the Standard Room, the Deluxe Room, the Superior Suite and the jacuzzi rooms side by side. When choosing, you can take into account the size of the room, the setting you want and the purpose of your visit.

The Standard Room is a more practical option for guests who want a comfortable stay and for whom extra space isn't the main requirement. The Deluxe Room offers a roomier setting, while the Superior Suite suits guests who prefer an especially large room. The jacuzzi options are aimed more at guests who want extra comfort and a different setting in the room.

When choosing, it's worth deciding in advance what matters most to you. If you just need a quiet, comfortable room, one of the regular double rooms may be enough. But if you want time in the room itself to be an important part of your break, a room with a private jacuzzi is the more appealing choice. The Jacuzzi room and the Jacuzzi Suite give you everything you need to relax and a private jacuzzi in one space.

Booking

Hotel Agava has only two rooms with a private jacuzzi, so booking ahead for your preferred date is recommended. Especially on Saturdays, days off, during holidays and on popular dates, finding a free room can be harder. If you're planning to celebrate an anniversary, a birthday or another important day, it's best to check availability a few days in advance.

If you're looking for a jacuzzi room for a couple in Tbilisi, Hotel Agava lets you choose between two different spaces. You can pick the Jacuzzi room with a King-size bed or the Jacuzzi Suite with a city view, and plan your break around the setting you prefer. In both options the jacuzzi is in your private space, which gives a couple the chance of a calm, comfortable break.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How many rooms have a jacuzzi?

Two — the Jacuzzi suite and the Super suite with jacuzzi. Both are for two guests.

Is the jacuzzi in the room or in a shared area?

The jacuzzi is inside the room. You do not share it with other guests or leave your room to use it.

How many guests can stay in a jacuzzi room?

Both jacuzzi rooms are designed for two guests. For larger parties, see the family rooms for 3–5 people.

How far in advance should I book?

There are only two jacuzzi rooms and they fill quickly at weekends, so book as early as you can — especially around holidays.

Can I pay by card?

No, payment is made on site, in cash only.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out are charged.

Does the hotel have a restaurant or a pool?

No. The hotel has no full-menu restaurant, swimming pool, spa or gym.
