A hotel in Tbilisi for business visits

When choosing a hotel for a business trip, what matters is not only how comfortable the room is but also the practical things that make planning a working day easier. Hotel Agava offers guests visiting Tbilisi on business comfortable rooms, free Wi-Fi, its own parking and a 24/7 reception. The hotel is located in Dighomi, on Akaki Beliashvili Street, and is a convenient choice for anyone who has to travel to different parts of the city during the day and wants to come back to a calm setting in the evening.

Free Wi-Fi

On a business visit, guests' needs often differ from those of a leisure trip. Much of the day may be spent at meetings, in an office, at an exhibition, at a training course or on other work, so what matters most at the hotel is a comfortable bed, a quiet setting, internet access and services that make everyday arrangements easier. Hotel Agava brings these together in one place and lets guests choose a room by the length of their visit and the comfort they want.

Rooms for two guests

Several types of room are available for one or two guests. For a short business visit, a practical option is the Standard Room, designed for two guests and offering the essentials for rest day to day. If you're staying in Tbilisi for just one or two nights and spending most of the day away from the hotel, this option may be perfectly enough. When you're back in the room, you can rest in peace, sort out work matters and get ready for the next day.

For guests who want more space after the working day, Hotel Agava has the Deluxe Room. The Deluxe Room is roomier than the Standard Room and especially convenient on a visit of several days. The extra space lets you store luggage, work things or a laptop more freely and feel more comfortable in the room. This can matter when you carry on working at the hotel in the evening or use the same space every day for several days.

If you prefer an even roomier setting, you can choose the Superior Suite. The Superior Suite is a good choice for guests for whom room size matters a great deal on a business visit. A larger space is especially pleasant on a longer stay, because the room is no longer just a place to sleep and is also used for work or rest at certain times of the day.

All of these rooms have a private bathroom, air conditioning and free Wi-Fi. A private bathroom adds comfort, since guests have everything they need right in the room. Air conditioning lets you keep the room at the temperature you like, and free internet is one of the most important things on a business visit.

At Hotel Agava, Wi-Fi is free both in the rooms and in the shared areas. This lets you answer work messages, check your email, prepare documents or join an online meeting from the hotel if you need to. If you have some free time between meetings, you can come back to the hotel and do part of your work from the room without having to find an extra workspace in the city.

Free parking

The hotel's own parking is another important advantage for business guests. If you're getting around Tbilisi in your own or a company car, being able to leave it in the hotel yard makes your daily schedule simpler. Driving to meetings during the day is often more convenient, and when you come back in the evening you don't have to look for a parking space separately. This is especially practical for anyone who has to be in different parts of the city during the working day.

Hotel Agava has a 24/7 reception, which is another important advantage on a business visit. Work schedules don't always fit standard hours, and arrival in Tbilisi often happens late in the evening or at night. If a flight is delayed, a meeting runs over or there's a hold-up on the road, the hotel reception is still available. This lets you plan your arrival time more freely and reach the staff even if you get back late.

Breakfast

Planning the morning properly matters on a business visit too. At Hotel Agava you can add breakfast, which is especially convenient for anyone who needs to get to a meeting or to work on time in the morning. Having breakfast at the hotel saves time and means you don't need to find a café or somewhere else before your day begins.

Laundry

If you're staying in Tbilisi for several days or longer, a laundry service is also available. This is especially practical for business guests who have to wear formal or work clothes over several days. A service like this lets you pack fewer things and not worry about keeping your clothes in order.

For anyone flying into Tbilisi, an airport transfer can also be arranged in advance. This is convenient when you're in the city for the first time, have a lot of luggage or simply want the journey from the airport to the hotel organised ahead of time. The same service can come in handy on the day you leave. You can see the terms for breakfast, the transfer and other services on the amenities page of Hotel Agava.

Not every guest on a business visit needs a conference room or a big business centre. In most cases the main requirements are a quiet room, stable internet, parking, flexible check-in and a comfortable rest after the working day. Hotel Agava is an especially practical choice for exactly this kind of visit. You can be at meetings during the day and come back in the evening to a place where you can rest and, if needed, carry on working.

On a visit of several days, room size is especially worth considering. For one night or a short visit the Standard Room may be enough, but if you're staying in Tbilisi for several days, extra space is often more comfortable. The Deluxe Room and the Superior Suite let you store luggage and work things freely and feel more comfortable in the room over several days.

Hotel Agava is in Tbilisi, in Dighomi, and brings together several practical things business guests need in one place. Free Wi-Fi, the hotel's own parking, a 24/7 reception, the option to add breakfast, a laundry service and an airport transfer make organising your visit easier. And rooms of different sizes let you choose the option that matches the length of your visit and the comfort you need.

If you're coming to Tbilisi on business and need a hotel where you can rest in peace after the working day, use the internet and park your car with ease, Hotel Agava is a practical choice. You can choose the type of room according to how many days you're staying and how much the extra space matters to you.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Is there a conference room?

No. The hotel has no conference room and no business centre. The focus is a comfortable room, internet, parking and a 24/7 reception.

Is the Wi-Fi good enough for video calls?

Wi-Fi is free and works in every room with no limits. The speed varies with the time of day and how busy the hotel is — if you have video calls, ask when you book and we will suggest a suitable room.

Can I check in late at night?

Yes. Reception is staffed 24 hours with a real person overnight, so you can check in at any hour.

Can I pay by card?

No, the hotel is settled in cash only. If you pay on a company card or need documentation, call +995 597 12 12 12 before booking.

How far is the airport?

30 to 40 minutes. The transfer is 70 GEL from the airport and 50 GEL back — per car, with four seats.

How quickly is laundry done?

In 12 hours, at 10 GEL per item. Something handed in during the morning is ready by the evening.

Is parking free, and how many spaces are there?

Yes, it's free for hotel guests. There are 11 spaces in the yard, so please let us know in advance if you're arriving by car.

How much is the airport transfer?

From the airport to the hotel 70 GEL, from the hotel to the airport 50 GEL. The price is per car, with four seats; the journey takes 30–40 minutes.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out are charged.

How much is laundry?

10 GEL per item, ready in 12 hours.
