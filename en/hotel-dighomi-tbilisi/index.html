A hotel on Beliashvili Street

Akaki Beliashvili Street is one of the main arteries of Dighomi. Hotel Agava sits at number 161, on a quieter stretch, with its own courtyard. The district is part of Didube. The courtyard also has a small seating area where you can sit and relax in the morning or evening.

Getting to the hotel

If you are driving, you will not need to look for a space — parking is in the hotel courtyard. We can also arrange an airport transfer: tell us your flight time in advance and a car will be waiting. For directions, call +995 597 12 12 12 — we know the area well and will talk you through it.

Getting here from the airport — every option

There are several ways in from Tbilisi airport and the prices differ sharply. Here is all of them, including the one that undercuts us:

  • Bus 337 — around 1 GEL, roughly 07:00 to 23:00, every 15–20 minutes. It runs towards the centre and the railway station, so you would continue from there by taxi
  • Bolt — usually 15–25 GEL to the centre, a 20–30 minute drive
  • Drivers touting in the arrivals hall — the price is not known in advance and depends on how well you negotiate
  • Our transfer — 70 GEL from the airport, 50 GEL back; per car, four seats. Details on the transfer page

Honestly: Bolt is cheaper than our transfer, and if cost is what matters it is a perfectly good choice. Our transfer sells something else — a price fixed before you land, a driver waiting for you inside the arrivals hall, and the same fare on a night flight. At three in the morning in an unfamiliar city, that is often worth the difference.

The drive from the airport to the hotel takes 30 to 40 minutes. Transfers should be booked one or two days ahead — call +995 597 12 12 12.

Why Dighomi rather than the centre

Plainly: Dighomi is not Old Tbilisi, and if your plan is to walk down Rustaveli every evening, staying in the centre will suit you better.

What Dighomi gives you instead is three things the centre cannot: a free space for the car in the hotel yard, a quiet night without bars or tourist noise, and a lower price for the same comfort.

If you are driving

With your own or a hired car, Dighomi is the logical choice. Hotel parking in the centre is often paid, limited or absent altogether — here there are 11 spaces in the yard, free for guests. See the parking page; because there are fewer spaces than rooms, tell us in advance if you are bringing a car.

It is convenient for leaving the city too — Mtskheta, Ananuri and the Georgian Military Highway are reached without crossing the centre first.

Who this does not suit

If you are in Tbilisi for two nights and want to spend all of it in the old town without a car, then honestly — look in the centre.

Dighomi is the right choice when you have a car, or when your business is on this side of the city: the US Embassy, the residential districts of Dighomi, or the road out of town.
