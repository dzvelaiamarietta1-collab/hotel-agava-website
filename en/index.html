A hotel in Tbilisi for a comfortable stay

Hotel Agava is a hotel in Tbilisi designed for couples and families as well as guests visiting on business. The hotel is located in Dighomi, on Akaki Beliashvili Street, and offers guests different types of rooms, free Wi-Fi, its own parking and a 24/7 reception. The choice of rooms lets you pick your accommodation by the number of guests, the purpose of your visit and the comfort you want. You can choose a practical, compact room for two, a roomier space, a family room or a room with a private jacuzzi.

Rooms for two guests

Hotel Agava has several different rooms for two guests. The Standard Room is a good choice for anyone who needs a clean, practical space for a short visit. If you'd like more free space, you can choose the Deluxe Room, and for an even roomier setting there's the Superior Suite. This choice is especially convenient when you're staying in Tbilisi for several days and room size is part of the extra comfort you're looking for.

Rooms with a private jacuzzi

For couples planning a break in a setting different from an ordinary hotel room, Hotel Agava has two rooms with a private jacuzzi. Both rooms are designed for two guests, and the jacuzzi is right inside the room. This means guests don't need to use a shared space and can spend their time in peace in their own room. The jacuzzi rooms are a good choice for an anniversary, a birthday, a romantic evening or simply when you want a change from your everyday surroundings. You can compare these rooms and choose the one that suits you on the jacuzzi rooms page.

Family rooms for 3–5 guests

Hotel Agava also offers several options for guests travelling with family. For three guests there's a family room that works both for two parents and a child and for three adults. For four guests you can choose a roomier family room, and for five guests the hotel has the Two-Room Suite. Having two separate rooms is especially practical for a large family, because everyone stays in one suite while each person has more personal space.

When travelling with family, a choice like this makes planning your stay much easier. There's no need to book several different rooms if you can find the right space for the whole family. This matters especially when travelling with children, when parents prefer to have every family member in one room. The Two-Room Suite, meanwhile, is good for anyone who wants to stay in one space but also values having separate rooms. You can see the different family accommodation options on the family hotel page.

Hotel Agava is a good choice for business visits too. If you're coming to Tbilisi for work meetings, a training course, a conference or a business trip of several days, it's especially important that the hotel has the essentials you need. Guests have free internet, which lets you work, answer emails or join an online meeting when you need to. The hotel's own parking is convenient when you're getting around by car, and the 24/7 reception lets you plan your arrival time more freely.

Business guests can also choose a room by the length of their visit and the space they want. If you mostly come back to the hotel in the evening, the Standard Room may be enough. If you're staying several days and need more space in the room, the Deluxe Room or the Superior Suite is the more comfortable choice. You can find more about this kind of stay on the business travellers page.

Breakfast

Guests at Hotel Agava can also make use of additional services. Breakfast is available at the hotel, which is especially convenient if you want to head out into the city quickly in the morning without looking for somewhere separate to eat. You can also use the airport transfer, which makes planning your arrival in Tbilisi and your departure easier. This service is especially useful for anyone visiting the city for the first time or travelling with luggage.

A hotel with a lift — on all four floors

A laundry service is also available, adding extra comfort on a longer visit. If you're staying at the hotel for several days or longer, a service like this makes everyday life easier and lets you travel with fewer things. A lift serves all four floors of Hotel Agava, which is especially convenient when moving luggage, travelling with children or for guests who find stairs uncomfortable.

Free parking

The hotel's own parking matters to anyone travelling around Tbilisi by car. Finding a parking space in the city often takes extra time, so being able to leave the car on the hotel grounds makes getting around day to day easier for guests. This advantage is especially convenient for families, for guests arriving with a lot of luggage and for anyone driving to business meetings around the city in their own car.

24/7 reception

A 24/7 reception lets guests plan their arrival at the hotel more freely. If your flight lands late, there's a delay on the road or you reach Tbilisi during the night, check-in is still available. This matters especially when travelling, because your arrival time can't always be pinned down exactly in advance. You can see the full list of the hotel's services and extras on the amenities page.

Location — Dighomi

Hotel Agava is in Tbilisi, in Dighomi, which can be convenient for anyone who wants to stay in relatively quiet surroundings. At the hotel you can plan both a short visit and a stay of several days. Guests can choose a room according to their specific needs — one type of accommodation doesn't have to suit everyone.

If there are two of you and you need a practical room, you can choose the Standard Room. For more space there are the Deluxe Room and the Superior Suite, and for a special break you can book a room with a private jacuzzi. For families there are spaces for three, four and five guests. And on a business visit, free internet, parking and the 24/7 reception make everyday arrangements simpler.

If you're looking for a hotel in Tbilisi where you can choose your room by the number of guests and the purpose of your visit, Hotel Agava brings several different options together in one place. You can compare the rooms in advance, taking into account how many of you there are, how much space you need and which extras matter to you. Once you've chosen the room you want, you can check available dates and book your stay directly with the hotel.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00. Early check-in or late check-out is possible by prior arrangement, subject to availability.

How do I pay?

Payment is made on site at the hotel — in cash only — cards are not accepted. No prepayment is required for online booking.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before arrival — call us or message us on WhatsApp with your booking number.

Is there parking?

Yes, the hotel has free, secure parking for guests — right in the courtyard.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is available as an extra — with a varied menu every morning. Mention it in your booking comment or arrange it with reception.

Are pets allowed?

Please contact us in advance about pets — we consider each case individually.
