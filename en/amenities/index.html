Hotel Agava services and extra comforts

When choosing a hotel, the room type and size aren't the only things that matter. How comfortable your stay is overall also depends a great deal on which additional services are available on site and how much the hotel makes everyday life easier. Hotel Agava offers guests free Wi-Fi, its own parking, the option to add breakfast, an airport transfer, a laundry service, a lift and a 24/7 reception. Some of the services are included in the stay, while others are available as extras at the guest's request.

Free Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi works both in the rooms and in the hotel's shared areas. This matters both to guests who come to relax and to anyone in Tbilisi on business who needs the internet for everyday work. From the hotel you can answer work emails, prepare documents, look up information or join an online meeting. If you plan to keep working during your visit, internet access becomes an especially important factor when choosing a hotel.

Business guests also benefit from being able to choose between several types of room at Hotel Agava. For a short visit a more compact room may be enough, while for a stay of several days a guest can choose a roomier option. The hotel's other practical services also make planning a business visit easier. You can find more information on the business travellers page.

Free parking

For guests arriving in their own car, free parking is available in Hotel Agava's yard. When getting around Tbilisi by car, parking is often an important issue, so being able to leave the car on the hotel grounds gives guests extra comfort. There's no need to look for a free space on the surrounding streets and leave the car far away.

Parking is especially practical for families. When travelling with children you often have more luggage, bags and other things you need, so being able to park close to the hotel makes arrival and departure much easier. It's also comfortable for anyone staying several days who plans to get around the city in their own car every day.

A hotel with a lift — on all four floors

The hotel's lift also makes family travel easier. A lift serves all four floors of Hotel Agava, so getting to your room with luggage is more comfortable. This matters especially when visiting with small children, when you may need to bring a pushchair, several bags or other things. The lift is also convenient for older guests and for anyone who finds using the stairs uncomfortable.

Hotel Agava has several different types of room for families, so you can choose a space by the number of guests. Whether there are three, four or five of you, you can pick a room that matches your family's specific needs. You can see the relevant options on the family hotel page.

24/7 reception

Reception at Hotel Agava is open 24 hours a day. This lets guests plan their arrival time more flexibly and reach the hotel staff even if they arrive late. When travelling, your arrival time can't always be pinned down exactly in advance. A delayed flight, a hold-up on the road or other circumstances can change your plans, so a 24/7 reception adds peace of mind in cases like these.

A round-the-clock reception also matters for guests who are already staying at the hotel. If you need help, information or to clear something up, you can reach the staff at any time of day or night. This is especially useful for guests visiting the city for the first time, who may need information about getting around or other practical matters.

Breakfast

At Hotel Agava you can also add breakfast. This is convenient for anyone who prefers to have breakfast at the hotel before starting the day and doesn't want to look for a café or somewhere else in the morning. The option to add breakfast is practical on both family and business visits. If you need to head into the city early or get to a work meeting in the morning, having breakfast on site saves time.

Laundry

On a visit of several days or longer, guests can also use the laundry service. This is especially useful for anyone staying in Tbilisi for a long time who needs to keep their everyday clothes in order. The laundry service is convenient for business guests and families alike, as it reduces the need to bring a large amount of clothing.

Airport transfer

If you're flying into Tbilisi, a transfer from the airport to the hotel can be arranged in advance. The transfer is a good choice for anyone coming to the city for the first time, travelling with a lot of luggage or who simply wants the journey from the airport to the hotel organised ahead of time. If needed, a transfer from the hotel to the airport can also be arranged, which makes planning your departure day easier.

Arranging a transfer in advance is especially convenient for night or early-morning flights, when you don't want to organise extra travel around the city. That way you know beforehand how you'll get to the hotel or the airport, and there's one less part of the journey to think about.

Hotel Agava focuses on practical comfort and doesn't list services that aren't actually available at the hotel. The hotel has no swimming pool, spa or gym. The main emphasis is on comfortable rooms, the everyday services you need, the hotel's own parking, the lift and staff who are available 24 hours a day.

This matters especially to guests who want to know exactly what they'll get before choosing a hotel. If your main requirements are a comfortable room, internet, parking, flexible check-in and the additional services you need, this is exactly the kind of stay Hotel Agava offers.

The services you need can vary with the type of visit. For a business guest, the internet, parking and the 24/7 reception may be especially important. For a family, the lift, breakfast, parking and the laundry service may matter more. And for guests arriving by plane, the airport transfer adds extra comfort.

Combining rooms and services lets you tailor your stay to your specific plans. You can first choose the type of room by the number of guests and the space you want, and then decide which additional services you'll need during your visit. With this approach, organising your hotel stay becomes simpler and easier to plan ahead.

If you're staying in Tbilisi for several days, you can add breakfast, the laundry service and other services you need. If you're arriving in your own car, free parking will make getting around each day easier. And if you're flying in, you can arrange a transfer in advance. Together with all these services, the 24/7 reception lets you plan your arrival and your stay at the hotel more flexibly.

Hotel Agava's services are designed so that guests can easily take care of their main practical needs during their stay. Free Wi-Fi, parking, a lift, the option to add breakfast, a laundry service, an airport transfer and a round-the-clock reception provide the comfort needed for many different kinds of visit.

To see current offers and booking terms, you can go to Hotel Agava's offers page. Once you've chosen a room, you can think about which additional services you'll need and arrange your hotel stay to suit your type of visit and your plans.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Does the hotel have a lift?

Yes. The hotel has four floors and the lift serves all of them.

Is parking free?

Yes, free for hotel guests. There are 11 spaces in the yard, so please tell us in advance if you are arriving by car.

Is the Wi-Fi charged?

No, Wi-Fi is free and works in every room and all common areas, with no time or device limit.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

No. Breakfast is ordered separately at 30 GEL per guest — a buffet served 08:00 to 11:00.

Is there a restaurant or a pool?

No. The hotel has no full-menu restaurant, swimming pool, spa or gym.

Can I leave my bags after check-out?

Unfortunately not — the hotel has no luggage room. If you are staying in the city that day, a late check-out is the better option.

How much is the airport transfer?

From the airport to the hotel 70 GEL, from the hotel to the airport 50 GEL. The price is per car, with four seats; the journey takes 30–40 minutes.

Can I pay by card?

No, payment is made on site, in cash only.

How much is breakfast?

30 GEL per guest, buffet from 08:00 to 11:00. Not included in the room price.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out are charged.

How much is laundry?

10 GEL per item, ready in 12 hours.

Is there a conference room?

No. The hotel has no conference room or business centre.

Can I smoke?

Smoking is not allowed in the rooms. You can smoke outside, in the covered part of the courtyard. There is no fine.
