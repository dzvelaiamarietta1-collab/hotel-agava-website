A family hotel in Tbilisi

When travelling with family, choosing a hotel often takes more care, because what matters is not only the number of beds but also how comfortable the room is and whether there's parking, a lift and other services that make staying with children easier. Hotel Agava in Tbilisi offers several types of room suited to family stays, designed for three to five guests. This lets you choose a room by the number of family members and avoid booking a space that's too small or, on the contrary, much bigger than you need.

For two parents and one child, a practical choice is the Family Room for 3 guests. This room is designed for three guests and lets a family stay comfortably in one space. This option is especially convenient when the child is small and the parents prefer the whole family to stay in one room. Staying in one room makes both everyday arrangements and looking after the child easier, especially in the evening and at night.

For a family of four, the more suitable option is the Family Room for 4 guests. The extra space lets four guests feel less restricted and store what they need more comfortably. When you travel with family there's usually more luggage, because along with clothes you may need the child's things, toys, food and other essentials. That's exactly why room size has practical importance and the number of beds alone isn't enough.

If your family is larger and you need to accommodate five guests, Hotel Agava has the Two-Room Suite. This option is especially convenient for a large family, because two separate rooms let parents and children stay in different spaces within one suite. This layout creates a calmer setting, especially when family members have different sleeping or resting routines. The children can rest in one room while the parents stay in the other, and everyone is still in the same suite.

Free parking

When choosing a family hotel, the details that make arrival and everyday life at the hotel easier matter a great deal. Guests at Hotel Agava have the hotel's own parking, which is especially convenient when travelling by car. When you travel with children you often bring several bags, suitcases and other things, so being able to park in the hotel yard makes arriving much easier. There's no need to look for a space on the street or carry luggage a long way.

Parking is also convenient on a visit of several days if you plan to get around Tbilisi in your own car. For a family, driving is often more practical, especially when you have small children or plan to visit several places in a day. When you come back to the hotel, you know there's a place for your car and no more time is lost looking for parking.

A hotel with a lift — on all four floors

The hotel building has a lift serving all four floors. This matters especially when moving around with luggage or small children. If you have a pushchair, heavy bags or several suitcases, the lift makes getting to your room much more comfortable. This detail can also matter a great deal to families with older relatives or guests who have difficulty getting around.

24/7 reception

Reception at Hotel Agava is open 24 hours a day, so you can plan your family's arrival time more freely. When travelling, especially with children, sticking exactly to a schedule set in advance isn't always easy. A delay on the road, a late flight or other circumstances can change your arrival time. In that case a 24/7 reception adds peace of mind, because you can reach the hotel staff at any time of day or night.

Breakfast

In the morning you can also add breakfast, a practical choice for families. When starting the day with children, it often matters not to have to find somewhere separate for breakfast. Having breakfast at the hotel lets you begin the morning more calmly and then plan your outing into the city. This is especially convenient for anyone staying in Tbilisi for several days who wants to make getting around each day simpler.

Laundry

On a visit of several days or longer, a laundry service is also available. This adds comfort for families, since clothes often need changing more frequently when travelling with children. The laundry service lets you pack fewer things or get clothes sorted on site when needed. You can see the additional services and terms on the amenities page.

When choosing a family room, it's best to consider the number of guests first and then decide how much extra space matters. For three guests, the Family Room for 3 guests is the most practical choice, as it lets a family stay comfortably in one space. For four guests, the Family Room for 4 guests offers a roomier setting, while for five guests the Two-Room Suite is especially convenient because its two separate rooms provide extra personal space.

The length of your visit is also worth considering when choosing a room. For one or two nights a relatively compact space is often perfectly enough, but on a family visit of several days a roomier room makes everyday life much easier. More space for luggage, room to move around freely and somewhere to put the children's things matter more in that case.

Hotel Agava is also a practical choice for families because the whole family can stay in one room. This lets you avoid booking two separate rooms and keeps family members from being split up. This is an important advantage especially when travelling with small children, since parents can stay close to them throughout the visit.

If you're coming to Tbilisi with your family and want a hotel where you can choose a room by the number of guests, Hotel Agava offers several different options. Family rooms for three and four guests, as well as the Two-Room Suite for five, let you choose the space that suits your family best. And the hotel's own parking, the lift, the 24/7 reception, the option to add breakfast and the laundry service make a family visit more comfortable and easier to plan.

If you've already settled on your dates, it's best to book a family room in advance. The number of rooms of each type is limited, and on especially busy days the free options can fill up quickly. Booking ahead lets you choose exactly the room that best matches your family's size, needs and the comfort you want.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How many guests does the largest room sleep?

The two-bedroom suite sleeps up to five and consists of two separate rooms. There are also family rooms for three and four guests.

Is parking free?

Yes. Parking is free and secure, in the hotel's own courtyard, with 11 spaces.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Breakfast is not included in the room rate — you order it separately. Add a note to your booking or arrange it with reception on arrival.

Can we check in at night?

Yes, reception is staffed 24 hours. Let us know in advance if you will arrive late and we will wait up.

Can an extra bed be added?

Whether an extra bed is possible depends on the room and the dates — call us on +995 597 12 12 12 and we will tell you exactly what is available for your stay.
