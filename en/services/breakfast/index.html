Hotel breakfast — the buffet

Breakfast at Hotel Agava is a buffet, served 08:00 to 11:00. It costs 30 GEL per guest, and coffee, tea and juice are included in that price.

Breakfast at a glance

What you need to know

30 GEL per guest

Not included in the room rate — you order it when booking or at the desk.

08:00 – 11:00

Three hours: early enough for a morning appointment, late enough for a slow start.

Help yourself

A buffet, not a set plate — you take what you want, as much as you want.

Coffee, tea and juice included

Drinks are not charged separately; they are part of the same 30 GEL.

Price and how to pay

Breakfast costs 30 GEL per person and is not included in the room rate. You can add it in two ways: mention it when you book, or arrange it with reception once you are here.

One detail worth knowing before you arrive, so there is no surprise: there is no children's discount and no age below which breakfast is free. It is 30 GEL for every guest.

When it is served

Breakfast opens at 08:00 and closes at 11:00, and is served in the hotel dining room.

If you have something on in the morning — an appointment at the US Embassy, for instance, which is a ten-minute walk from here — an 08:00 opening means a 09:00 appointment is comfortable rather than rushed.

What is on the table

The buffet is laid out the same way every morning. Here is the full list, so you know what to expect:

  • Eggs — boiled, fried or as an omelette
  • Several kinds of cheese
  • Ham and sausage
  • Bread and pastries — including croissants
  • Vegetables — cucumber, tomato and seasonal salads
  • Olives
  • Yoghurt and milk
  • Cereal — cornflakes
  • Jam, honey and butter
  • Seasonal fruit
  • Coffee, tea and juice

For vegetarians

There is a vegetarian option, and it makes a full breakfast rather than a consolation plate: salads, several cheeses, eggs, vegetables, olives, yoghurt, pastries, jam and honey, seasonal fruit and cereal.

What we cannot do

Two things to know in advance, particularly if you have an early flight:

  • We cannot serve breakfast before 08:00. If you are leaving at 07:00, you will miss it
  • There is no packed option. We do not prepare breakfast to take away

If you are leaving early, it is better not to order breakfast for that day at all — that way you keep the 30 GEL. Our airport transfer runs at any hour.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does breakfast cost?

Breakfast is 30 GEL per guest. Coffee, tea and juice are included in that price.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

No. Breakfast is charged separately and ordered either when you book or at reception after you arrive.

Is there a discount for children?

No. There is no children's discount and no age below which breakfast is free — it is 30 GEL for every guest.

What time is breakfast?

Breakfast is served from 08:00 to 11:00 in the hotel dining room.

I have an early flight — can I eat earlier?

Unfortunately not. Breakfast opens at 08:00 and we cannot serve before that, and we do not prepare a packed option either.

Is there a vegetarian option?

Yes. The buffet includes salads, several kinds of cheese, eggs, vegetables, olives, yoghurt, pastries, fruit and cereal.

Is it a buffet or served to the table?

It is a buffet — you help yourself, with no limit on how much you take.
