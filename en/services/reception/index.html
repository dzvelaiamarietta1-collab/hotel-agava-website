There really is someone there at night

Plenty of small hotels advertise "24/7 reception" and what they mean is a bell by the door and somebody who has to be woken up. That is not the case here: a member of staff is on the desk overnight.

It matters on the night you land at two in the morning from the airport, or when you are driving in after a long day and cannot say exactly when you will get here. You do not need to warn anybody or apologise for the hour — you walk in and check in.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00.

Early check-in and late check-out are both possible, but they are charged and depend on how full the hotel is that day. Call +995 597 12 12 12 for the exact amount — we will check your dates and tell you straight away.

What languages we speak

Staff speak Georgian, English, Russian and Turkish. That combination is not a given in Tbilisi — Turkish in particular is rare — so if you are arriving from Turkey, language will not be the difficult part of the trip.

What reception can help with

  • Calling a taxi — especially in the morning when you are in a hurry
  • Booking a tour — Mtskheta, Kazbegi, Kakheti
  • Booking a restaurant — or just telling you which one is worth the trip
  • Local questions — where the nearest pharmacy or supermarket is

What reception cannot do

Three things worth knowing before you arrive:

  • There is no luggage room. We cannot hold bags after check-out — so if you are staying in the city that day, a late check-out is the more practical answer
  • No currency exchange. You will need to find an exchange office nearby
  • No card payment. The hotel is settled in cash only, so bring lari with you

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

I am arriving at night — can I still check in?

Yes. Reception is staffed 24 hours with a real person on the desk overnight, so you can check in at any hour.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 13:00 and check-out is by 12:00.

Can I have an early check-in or late check-out?

Both are possible, but they are charged and depend on how full the hotel is that day. Call +995 597 12 12 12 for the exact amount.

Can I leave my bags after check-out?

Unfortunately not — the hotel does not have a luggage room. If you are staying in the city that day, a late check-out is the better option.

What languages do the staff speak?

Georgian, English, Russian and Turkish.

Can I pay by card?

No, the hotel is settled in cash only. There is no currency exchange service either.

Will reception help me book a taxi or a tour?

Yes. Reception is happy to call a taxi and to help you book tours or restaurants.
