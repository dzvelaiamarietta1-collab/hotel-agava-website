What you need to know

10 GEL per item

Priced by the item, so a shirt, a pair of trousers and a dress each cost the same.

Ready in 12 hours

Arrange at reception

Made for longer stays

One week's suitcase can carry you through a three-week trip.

Price and turnaround

Laundry costs 10 GEL per item. It is priced per item rather than by weight, which means you know the total before you hand anything over — just count the pieces.

Turnaround is 12 hours. In practice, clothes handed in during the morning are back the same evening, and anything given in at night is ready the following morning.

How to arrange it

There is nothing to book and nobody to call in advance. Hand your things in at reception, where somebody is on the desk through the night — so there is no cut-off time to work around.

If you need something back before you leave, say so when you hand it in, so it fits inside the 12-hour window and nobody has to rush at the end.

Who tends to use it

  • Longer stays. You packed for a week and you are staying three — so you wash halfway through
  • Families with children. Clothes get through twice as fast with children along
  • Business trips. When you need a clean shirt for a meeting and do not want to go shopping for one

Worth asking reception about

Individual items — a coat, a suit, or anything in a delicate fabric — are checked case by case. Just show the piece at reception before you hand it in and they will tell you straight away.
