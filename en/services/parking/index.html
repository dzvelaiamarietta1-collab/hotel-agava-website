Free hotel parking — Tbilisi, Dighomi

Hotel Agava has 11 parking spaces in its own yard, free for anyone staying at the hotel. The area is covered by cameras and watched by night security, and you can drive in at any hour.

Parking at a glance

What you need to know

11 spaces in the yard

Parking is on hotel grounds — no hunting for a spot on the street.

Open 24 hours

The gate is not locked. You can drive in at three in the morning.

Large vehicles fit

SUVs and minibuses fit easily. A tour coach can be parked too.

Free for guests

No nightly parking charge and no add-on for the stay.

How many spaces there are

There are 11 spaces in the yard and 21 rooms in the hotel. If every guest arrived by car on the same night, there would not be room for everyone — so we would rather say that here than have you find out on arrival.

In practice it rarely becomes a problem, because many guests arrive without a car. Still, tell us in advance if you are driving — add a note to your booking or call +995 597 12 12 12.

Who parks free

Parking is free for hotel guests only. This is not a public car park and we do not take vehicles from outside.

For guests there is no charge of any kind — not per night, not for the stay.

How secure it actually is

So that you have an accurate picture: the yard is covered by cameras, lit at night, and security staff are on duty.

At the same time, the yard is not fenced and the gate is not locked overnight — which is exactly why you can drive in whenever you arrive. So this is not a sealed, gated car park; it is a lit, camera-covered yard under the eye of security. We would still not leave valuables visible in the car, but that is true of any car park anywhere.

Big vehicles and groups

SUVs and minibuses fit without trouble, and there is no height restriction, because the parking is an open yard rather than an underground garage.

A tour coach can be parked as well. If you are travelling as a group, that is something central Tbilisi hotels almost never offer — call ahead and we will arrange it.

Arriving at night

You can drive into the yard at any hour, and there is a real person on the desk through the night, so nobody has to be woken up and nothing has to be arranged in advance.

What we do not have

There is no EV charger at the hotel. If you are driving an electric car, you will need to charge at a public station nearby.

Why this matters in Dighomi

In central Tbilisi, hotel parking is often paid, limited, or simply absent, and finding a space on the street becomes an evening ritual. That is one of the main reasons drivers choose Dighomi.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Is parking free?

Yes, parking is completely free for hotel guests. It is not a public car park — we do not take vehicles from outside.

How many spaces are there?

There are 11 spaces in the hotel yard. Since the hotel has 21 rooms, please tell us in advance if you are arriving by car.

Do I need to reserve a space?

It is a good idea. Add a note to your booking saying you are driving, or call +995 597 12 12 12.

Will an SUV or minibus fit?

Yes. The parking is an open yard, so there is no height restriction. A tour coach can be parked too — call ahead in that case.

Can I drive in at night?

Yes. The gate is not locked and the yard is accessible 24 hours. There is also a member of staff on reception all night.

Is the yard fenced?

No, the yard is not fenced and the gate is not locked at night. It is, however, covered by cameras, lit after dark and watched by security staff.

Is there an EV charger?

No, the hotel does not have an electric vehicle charger.
