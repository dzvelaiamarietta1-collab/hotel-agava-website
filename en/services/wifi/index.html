Free Wi-Fi in the hotel — Tbilisi

Wi-Fi at Hotel Agava is free and works in every room and all common areas. There is no extra charge, no time limit and no cap on the number of devices.

Wi-Fi at a glance

What you need to know

Completely free

Internet is part of the room rate — no separate tariff, no daily fee.

In every room

The network covers the rooms and the common areas alike.

Password at reception

You are given it at check-in — no sign-up form and no SMS code.

No limits

No time cap, and connect as many devices as you like.

Where it works

The network covers every room and the hotel's common areas, including the dining room where breakfast is served.

Getting connected

You are given the password at reception when you check in. There is no registration form, no account to create and no SMS code to wait for — you pick the network, type the password and you are online.

If the network does not appear or the password will not take, call reception at any hour — there is someone on the desk overnight.

No extra charge

Some hotels sell internet as a separate line item or ration it in free minutes. Not here: Wi-Fi is part of the room rate, there is no time limit, and the number of devices is not capped — phone, laptop and tablet at once is fine.

If you need it for work

Honestly: the speed varies with the time of day and how full the hotel is. That is true of any hotel, and we would rather say it than promise a number.

If the main point of your trip is remote work and you have video calls scheduled, ask us when you book — reception will tell you which rooms are better in that respect, so there are no surprises when you arrive.
