Airport transfer — Tbilisi

Transfer between Tbilisi International Airport and the hotel — 70 GEL on arrival, 50 GEL going back. The price is per car, not per person, and the car seats four. The driver meets you in the arrivals hall.

Transfer at a glance

What you need to know

70 GEL / 50 GEL

Airport to hotel is 70 GEL. Hotel to airport is 50 GEL. Per car, not per head.

Four seats

The fare is for the car, so it costs the same whether one of you travels or four.

Same price at night

We run night flights too, and the rate does not change after dark.

Met in arrivals

The driver waits for you inside — no searching, no negotiating a fare.

The price

  • Airport to hotel — 70 GEL
  • Hotel to airport — 50 GEL

The fare is per car, not per passenger. There are four seats, so four of you travelling costs the same as one. The drive takes 30 to 40 minutes.

How the pickup works

The driver meets you in the arrivals hall — you are not calling anyone, walking out to a car park, or agreeing a price with a stranger at one in the morning. If your flight is delayed, the driver waits.

How to book it

Order the transfer one or two days ahead. Call +995 597 12 12 12 or message us on WhatsApp with:

  • Arrival date and time
  • Flight number
  • Number of passengers

Night flights

We run transfers for night arrivals and the price does not change — it is the same 70 GEL at three in the morning.

You do not need to worry about checking in either: reception is staffed 24 hours and someone will be there when you arrive.

Payment

The transfer is paid in cash only. We do not accept cards, so have the fare with you before you get in the car.

What we do not do

  • Tbilisi airport only. We do not drive to Kutaisi airport or to the railway station
  • No child seat. If you are travelling with a small child, plan around that

The alternatives, honestly

A transfer is not the only way in from the airport, and it is not the cheapest. You can also:

  • Use Bolt — usually cheaper than our transfer
  • Take bus 337 — around 1 GEL, but it runs towards the centre, so you would continue from there

So when is the transfer worth it? In three situations: the price is fixed and known before you land, the driver is waiting inside for you, and a night arrival costs the same as a daytime one. If it is your first time in Georgia, or you are landing at 3am with family and luggage, that certainty is usually what you are actually paying for.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How much is the airport transfer?

70 GEL from the airport to the hotel and 50 GEL from the hotel to the airport. The price is per car, not per person.

How many people fit?

The car seats four. The fare is the same whether one of you travels or four.

Do you have a child seat?

No, we do not have a child seat. Please plan around that if you are travelling with a small child.

How far in advance should I book?

One or two days ahead. Call +995 597 12 12 12 with your flight number, date, time and number of passengers.

Where does the driver meet me?

In the arrivals hall. If your flight is delayed, the driver waits.

Do you run transfers for night flights?

Yes, and the price does not change — the night rate is the same as the daytime one.

Do you drive to Kutaisi airport?

No. The transfer covers Tbilisi International Airport only. We do not drive to the railway station either.

Can I pay by card?

No, the transfer is paid in cash only.

How long is the drive to the airport?

About 30 to 40 minutes, depending on traffic.
