Hotel offers and direct booking in Tbilisi

When booking a hotel in Tbilisi, what matters is not only the price of the room but also what you get under the chosen terms and which services you might need on top. Hotel Agava offers different types of room for two guests as well as for families and larger groups. And when you book directly, you can agree specific dates, the number of guests and any extra requests with the hotel itself. This is especially convenient when you want to know in advance exactly which room suits your needs.

Rooms for two guests

If your main priority is economical, practical accommodation, you can choose the Standard Room for two guests. This option is good for a short visit, a business trip or when you're coming back to the hotel mainly to rest and a large space isn't your main requirement. If you'd like more free space, there's the Deluxe Room, and for an even roomier setting you can choose the Superior Suite.

Rooms with a private jacuzzi

If you're looking for a hotel to celebrate a special day, take a look at the jacuzzi rooms in Tbilisi. Hotel Agava has two rooms with a private jacuzzi, and both are designed for two guests. When choosing, you can consider the bed, the view and the setting you want. One has a King-size bed, the other has 30 m² and a city view. Rooms like these are often in demand for anniversaries, birthdays or other important dates, so it's especially important to check availability in advance.

Family rooms for 3–5 guests

Hotel Agava has several family rooms of different sizes, designed for three to five guests. For three guests you can choose a more compact family room, for four guests there's a roomier option, and for five guests you can choose the Two-Room Suite. You can compare rooms by the number of family members on the family hotel page. A choice like this lets you accommodate the whole family in one space and reduces the need to book an extra room.

Free Wi-Fi

When judging the value of a room, looking only at the starting price isn't always enough. It's important to consider what's included in the stay and what you might need on top. At Hotel Agava, Wi-Fi and the hotel's parking are free for guests. These two services are especially practical for families, for guests visiting on business and for anyone getting around Tbilisi in their own car.

As extras, you can add breakfast, arrange an airport transfer and use the laundry service. If you're flying into Tbilisi, a transfer arranged in advance makes organising the journey easier. And on a visit of several days or longer, the laundry service is especially convenient. You can find detailed information about all the services and current terms on the amenities page.

Booking

One of the main advantages of booking directly is how simple communication is. When you contact the hotel directly, you can check exactly which room is free on your preferred dates, how many guests can be accommodated and which option suits your needs. This matters especially on a family visit, when the number of guests and beds needs to be taken into account correctly.

Direct communication is also convenient when choosing a jacuzzi room. If you can't decide which option suits you better, you can find out in advance which room is available and how they differ. The same goes for comparing the Deluxe Room and the Superior Suite, when the main question is room size and free space.

When booking directly, you can also get information about additional services in advance. For example, if you need breakfast, an airport transfer or the laundry service, you can find out the terms on which each is available. This will help you plan the overall budget for your visit and your daily plans more precisely.

Room availability at Hotel Agava changes depending on the specific dates. So if you already know exactly when you're arriving and leaving, it's best to check free rooms in advance. This applies especially to room types of which there are only a few. Family rooms and jacuzzi rooms can be booked up faster during busy periods, so waiting until the last minute isn't always a good idea.

On Saturdays, days off, during holidays and on other popular dates, demand for rooms is usually higher. If you're travelling for a specific event, an anniversary or a birthday, planning ahead will help you get the room you want. The same goes for a family visit, since the choice of rooms suitable for a particular number of guests may be limited.

When choosing a room, it's also important to consider the length of your visit. For one or two nights the Standard Room may be perfectly enough, but for a stay of several days the Deluxe Room or the Superior Suite may turn out to be more comfortable thanks to the extra space. And when travelling with family, it's best to focus on the number of guests and the room layout.

Free parking

If you're travelling by car, free parking is an important advantage. It saves you from looking for a free space on the street and lets you park on the hotel grounds. If you're flying in, arranging a transfer in advance may be more convenient. That's why, when judging the final cost and comfort of a room, it's a good idea to consider the whole visit together.

Booking directly is especially useful when your requirements go beyond the standard choice. For example, if you want the whole family to stay in one room, need a specific type of bed, a private jacuzzi or an extra service, contacting the hotel directly will give you more accurate information. That way you're not making your decision based only on a photo of the room and the starting price.

Hotel Agava brings together several accommodation options for different kinds of visit. For two guests you can choose the Standard Room, the Deluxe Room, the Superior Suite or a jacuzzi room. For families there are spaces designed for three to five guests. And adding services lets you fit your stay to your plans more easily.

When choosing a room, it's best to consider how many of you there are, how many days you're staying, how much a spacious room matters to you and whether you need any additional services. It also matters whether you're travelling by car and what time you plan to arrive at the hotel. Taking all these factors into account together will help you choose a room that better matches your real needs.

If you've already settled on your dates, you can check room availability in advance and confirm the terms that matter to you directly with Hotel Agava. That way it will be easier to choose both the type of room and the additional services, and to plan your stay in Tbilisi ahead of time.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How much is a room?

Rates change with the season and the date. Call +995 597 12 12 12 or message us on WhatsApp with your dates and number of guests, and we will give you the exact price.

Is prepayment required?

No. No online prepayment is needed to hold a booking — payment is taken at the hotel.

Can I pay by card?

No, payment is in cash only. Please bring lari with you.

Is breakfast included in the rate?

No. Breakfast is ordered separately and costs 30 GEL per guest — a buffet served 08:00 to 11:00.

Is parking charged?

No, parking is free for hotel guests. There are 11 spaces in the yard, so please tell us in advance if you are arriving by car.

Is booking direct cheaper?

Booking direct removes the intermediary and means your questions get answered immediately. For the exact terms on your dates, give us a call.

How much is the airport transfer?

From the airport to the hotel 70 GEL, from the hotel to the airport 50 GEL. The price is per car, with four seats; the journey takes 30–40 minutes.

How much is breakfast?

30 GEL per guest, buffet from 08:00 to 11:00. Not included in the room price.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out are charged.

How much is laundry?

10 GEL per item, ready in 12 hours.
