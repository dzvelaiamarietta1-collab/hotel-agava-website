Roughly 800 metres from the embassy

The Embassy of the United States in Tbilisi is on George Balanchine Street. It is roughly 800 metres from Hotel Agava — a 10–15 minute walk. That means you are not dependent on morning traffic at all: you simply walk out and go.

Getting to the embassy

Walking is the most predictable option — you will not be caught out by traffic. If the weather is against you or you are carrying a bag of documents, reception will help you call a taxi and can talk you through the route; we know the area well.

Do still leave a margin before your appointment: entering the embassy involves a queue and a security check, which usually takes longer than getting there.

  • Lux — for two guests, with more space

  • Two-bedroom suite — up to 5 guests

Every option is listed on the rooms page.

Parking and the 24/7 reception

Both matter if you are driving in from the regions: parking is free and in the hotel courtyard, and reception is staffed 24 hours, so you can check in even if you arrive late at night.

The morning of your appointment

Consular services run Monday to Friday and visits are at an appointed time. The exact hour and the instructions are in your appointment confirmation — check them on the embassy's official site, because the rules change.

The practical arithmetic: breakfast opens at 08:00 and the embassy is a ten-minute walk away, so a 09:00 appointment leaves you time to eat and still set off early.

Two things to know so the morning does not go wrong: we cannot serve breakfast before 08:00 and there is no packed option, and the hotel has no luggage room, so bags travel with you after check-out. If your appointment is after 12:00, it is worth arranging a late check-out — it is charged, but the room stays yours. Call +995 597 12 12 12 or message us on WhatsApp.

Staying the night before

Some of our guests come from the regions precisely so they can reach a morning appointment on time. In that case arriving the night before is the calmest option — the risk of being delayed on the road disappears.

Three things matter for that, and we have all three: reception staffed 24 hours, so a night arrival is not a problem; free parking in the yard if you are driving; and a quiet district where you will actually sleep the night before an interview.

If several members of a family have appointments, we have family rooms for 3 to 5 guests, so nobody needs to book separately.

What to check in advance

The rules for entering the embassy — what you may bring in, where phones are left, how early to arrive — change over time, so we deliberately do not print figures here that might mislead you.

The only reliable source is the embassy's own page. The distance from the hotel, at least, is such that if you have forgotten something, going back for it and returning fits inside twenty minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How far is the hotel from the US Embassy?

Hotel Agava is roughly 800 metres from the Embassy of the United States — a 10–15 minute walk. You do not need a car or a taxi.

Can I check in at night before an early interview?

Yes. Reception is staffed 24 hours, so you can check in at any time — including late at night if you are travelling in from the regions the day before.

Is parking free?

Yes. The hotel has free secure parking in its own courtyard, with 11 spaces.

Can you accommodate a family if several of us have interviews?

Yes. There are family rooms for three and four guests, and a two-bedroom suite sleeping up to five, so a family can stay together.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Breakfast is not included in the room rate — you order it separately. Add a note to your booking or arrange it with reception on arrival.
