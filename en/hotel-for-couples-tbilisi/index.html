A hotel for couples in Tbilisi

When choosing a hotel for a couple, everyone has different wishes and priorities. For some, a quiet, comfortable room matters most, others prefer more space, and for a special day the choice often falls on a room with a private jacuzzi. Hotel Agava offers several different options for two guests, which lets you choose a room according to the purpose of your visit, the setting you want and your comfort. You can choose a practical room for a short visit, a roomier space or a jacuzzi room made for a special break.

Rooms for two guests

If you're staying in Tbilisi for just one or a few days and what matters most is a clean, quiet and comfortable room, you can choose the Standard Room. This option is especially convenient for couples who spend most of the day in the city and come back to the hotel mainly to rest. The room provides the essential comforts and is a good choice for anyone for whom extra space isn't the main requirement.

If you'd like more free space in the room, the Deluxe Room offers a roomier setting. This choice can be especially convenient on a visit of several days, when you spend more time in the hotel room and the size of the space matters more. The extra room is handy for storing luggage too and makes the whole setting feel more relaxed. For a couple who want more space than a standard room, the Deluxe Room is a practical choice.

For an even roomier setting there's the Superior Suite. It's one of the most spacious of the regular rooms for two guests. The Superior Suite especially suits those who consider room size and free space important during a hotel stay. A room like this is good both for a break of several days and for when you simply want to spend time in more comfortable surroundings.

Rooms with a private jacuzzi

If you're choosing a hotel to celebrate a special day, Hotel Agava has two rooms with a private jacuzzi. The Jacuzzi room (Super Suite with Jacuzzi) stands out for its King-size bed, while the Jacuzzi Suite (deluxe room with a jacuzzi) stands out for its 30 m² and city view. Both rooms are designed for two guests, and in both the jacuzzi is right inside the room. So there's no need to go to a shared area or share it with other guests.

A room with a private jacuzzi is a good choice for an anniversary, a birthday, a break after the wedding or simply for spending a different kind of evening. At times like these, what matters is not only staying at a hotel but also the setting of the room itself. When the jacuzzi is right in your private space, you can spend your time in peace and plan your break the way you want.

The Jacuzzi room, with a King-size bed, costs 400 GEL. This option is especially convenient for couples for whom a private jacuzzi and a calm setting are what matter most. The Jacuzzi Suite, meanwhile, is for those who want the same kind of break along with a city view and a price of 300 GEL. This is exactly the difference to keep in mind when choosing.

If you're not sure which jacuzzi room better matches your needs, you can find a detailed comparison of both options on the jacuzzi rooms page. There you can weigh up the bed, the view and the price and decide which option you prefer.

Free Wi-Fi

Additional comforts at the hotel also make a relaxing stay easier for a couple. Guests at Hotel Agava have free Wi-Fi, which you can use for personal needs as well as for work. If you're coming by car, you can use the hotel's own parking, which saves you from looking for a free space on the street.

24/7 reception

The hotel also has a 24/7 reception. This is especially convenient for couples who arrive in Tbilisi late or plan to reach the hotel later after their evening plans. With reception open around the clock, you can plan your arrival more freely and contact the staff at any time if you need to.

Breakfast

If you'd like, you can also add breakfast. This is comfortable for anyone who prefers to start the morning calmly and have breakfast before leaving the hotel. If you're flying into Tbilisi, an airport transfer can also be arranged in advance. This is especially practical when you're in the city for the first time, have a lot of luggage or want the journey from the airport to the hotel organised ahead of time.

You can see the full list of Hotel Agava's services on the amenities page. When choosing a room, it's a good idea to consider not only its size and type but also which additional services you'll need during your visit.

If you're staying in Tbilisi as a couple for several days, room size may become more important. For a short visit the Standard Room is often perfectly enough, but for a relatively longer stay the Deluxe Room or the Superior Suite gives you a more relaxed setting. And if the main purpose of your visit is to relax and spend special time together, the Jacuzzi room or the Jacuzzi Suite may be the better choice.

When choosing a room, you can start by deciding what matters most to you. If you need practical, comfortable accommodation, the Standard Room is a good option. If you want more space, you can choose the Deluxe Room or the Superior Suite. And if you're planning a special evening and a private jacuzzi matters to you, you can choose one of the two jacuzzi rooms.

Location — Dighomi

Hotel Agava is in Tbilisi, in Dighomi, and brings together several different types of room for couples. This lets you compare regular rooms as well as roomier options and rooms made for a special break, all within one hotel. You can decide based on your budget, the room size and the purpose of your visit.

If you have a birthday, an anniversary or another important date planned, it's best to book a jacuzzi room in advance, because the hotel has only two rooms of this type. Especially on Saturdays, days off and popular dates, the number of free rooms can shrink quickly. Booking ahead lets you secure the option you want in good time.

For a regular visit, the Standard Room, the Deluxe Room and the Superior Suite give you a more flexible choice. You can take into account how long you're staying, how much a spacious room matters to you and what kind of setting you prefer. That way you can choose the room at Hotel Agava that best fits your plans and make your time together in Tbilisi more comfortable to plan.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How many jacuzzi rooms are there?

Two — the Jacuzzi suite and the Super suite with jacuzzi. Both are for two guests and the jacuzzi is inside the room.

Can the room be decorated for a special occasion?

We do not offer a fixed package, but tell us in the booking comment or by phone and we will arrange what we can.

Is a late check-out possible?

Check-out is until 12:00. A later departure can be arranged in advance, depending on how full the hotel is that day.

Is breakfast included in the room rate?

Breakfast is not included in the room rate — you order it separately. Add a note to your booking or arrange it with reception on arrival.

Can I pay by card?

No, payment is made on site, in cash only.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out are charged.
